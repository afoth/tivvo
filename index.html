Tivvo

A player for your own IPTV playlists on iPhone and iPad.

What Tivvo is

Tivvo plays M3U playlists that you add yourself, with a programme guide when your playlist provides one. Tivvo does not include, sell or recommend any channels, streams or subscriptions. What you can watch depends entirely on the playlist you add and on your right to use it.

Getting started

  1. Open Tivvo and choose Add Playlist.
  2. Give it a name and paste the playlist address (an M3U or M3U8 link) from your provider.
  3. Tivvo loads the channels. If the playlist names a guide (XMLTV), the guide loads too.

Common questions

A channel says "Channel unavailable". The stream did not answer. Tap Retry, and check that the playlist still works at your provider.

A channel says the format isn't supported. The stream uses a format the device cannot play.

There is no guide. The playlist has no guide address, or the guide does not list that channel. In Settings you can refresh the guide and shift its times.

Going back in time does not work. Catch-up needs a provider that offers it for that channel.

How do I free up space? Settings → Clear cache. Your playlists and favourites are kept.

Contact

Questions and problem reports: open an issue on GitHub. Please do not post your playlist address there; it is usually personal.
