Privacy Policy

Tivvo for iPhone and iPad. Effective 18 November 2026.

Summary

Tivvo does not collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer of Tivvo receives nothing from the app.

What stays on your device

  • The playlists you add. Their addresses are kept in the device's Keychain.
  • Channels, the programme guide, favourites, recently watched channels and where you stopped in a programme.
  • Your settings and cached channel logos.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes it. Settings → Clear cache removes the downloaded guide and logos.

Connections Tivvo makes

Tivvo connects only to the addresses in the playlists you add: the playlist itself, its programme guide, channel logos and the streams you play. Those servers are run by your provider, not by the developer of Tivvo. As with any connection, they can see your IP address and what you request. Their handling of that information is covered by your provider's own privacy policy.

Many playlists use addresses without encryption (http). Tivvo allows them because providers require it; such connections can be read by others on the same network.

Diagnostics

Tivvo writes technical log entries on your device to help find problems. Playlist addresses are removed from them. They are not sent anywhere by Tivvo.

Children

Tivvo is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
